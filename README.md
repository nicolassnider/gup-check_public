# GupCheck

**The consensus of black belts, the decision of the Sabum-Nim.** 🥋

![GupCheck Cover](public/gupcheck-cover.png)

GupCheck is the definitive digital platform designed exclusively for ITF Taekwondo schools. It revolutionizes how instructors evaluate and track the progress of colored belt (Gup) students, transforming a traditionally chaotic process into a seamless, professional experience.

## 🥋 The Challenge

As exam dates approach, evaluating dozens of students can become overwhelming:

- **Scattered Feedback:** Notes taken on loose papers get lost, and WhatsApp messages with critical corrections are easily forgotten.
- **Decision Fatigue:** The Sabum-Nim (Head Instructor) is forced to make crucial promotion decisions based on fragmented comments or pure memory.
- **Inefficient Meetings:** Countless hours are spent trying to reach a consensus on who is truly ready to test.

## 🚀 The GupCheck Solution

GupCheck empowers your Dojang by centralizing pre-evaluations into one intuitive digital tool.

Right from their smartphones, any Black Belt can observe a student during class and instantly submit structured, technical feedback on their **Tuls (Forms)**, **Matsogi (Sparring)**, and **Theory**.

All this vital information is immediately consolidated into an exclusive, easy-to-read dashboard for the Sabum-Nim.

## ✨ Key Benefits

- **Data-Driven Decisions:** The Head Instructor no longer decides in isolation. See the real consensus and technical insights from your entire Black Belt body on a single screen.
- **Time Optimization:** Eliminate the need for lengthy evaluation meetings and say goodbye to lost notes.
- **Personalized Student Tracking:** Pinpoint exactly what each student needs to improve (e.g., "Needs to review the meaning of their belt color") and adjust training in the crucial final weeks before an exam.
- **Elevated Professionalism:** Showcase a modern, organized, and professional image to your students and their parents, strengthening your school's identity and reputation.

## 📱 How It Works

1. **Schedule a Simulation:** The Sabum-Nim sets a date for an exam simulation a few weeks before the official test.
2. **Evaluate on the Go:** During the simulation, Black Belts use their phones to evaluate assigned students in real-time, scoring specific techniques and adding notes.
3. **Review & Decide:** The system automatically compiles the data. The Sabum-Nim reviews the clear metrics and makes informed final decisions on who advances to the official exam.
